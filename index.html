EV chargers and road trips, without the range anxiety.

JUICE is an iPhone app that finds chargers near you, plans road trips around your car's real battery, and keeps your next stop, arrival time and battery on the Lock Screen while you drive with any navigation app.

  • Chargers from public data sources, with filters for plug type, speed and network.
  • Trip plans that learn how your car actually uses energy.
  • Optional Tesla link (Pro) to read your live battery and send stops to the car.
  • No JUICE account, no ads, no tracking. Your data stays on your phone.
