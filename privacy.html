Privacy Policy

Effective November 10, 2026

JUICE ("the app") is made by Hunter Davis, an independent developer ("I", "me"). This policy explains what the app uses, why, and where it goes. The short version: there is no sign-up with me and no password (the optional JUICE account is Sign in with Apple, section 7), no ads, no analytics or tracking tools, and almost everything stays on your iPhone.

  • No sign-up with me and no passwords. The optional JUICE account is Sign in with Apple (section 7).
  • No ads, and no third-party analytics, crash-reporting or tracking SDKs.
  • I do not sell your data or share it for advertising. JUICE does not track you across other companies' apps or websites, so it never asks for tracking permission.
  • Unless you sign in to the optional JUICE account (section 7), JUICE keeps no database about you: your trips, cars, drives and settings are saved on your device.

1. Location

JUICE uses your precise location in these ways:

  • While using the app: to show chargers near you and to start trips from where you are.
  • During a trip you started: if you allow "Always", JUICE keeps using location in the background only while that trip is running, so your next stop, arrival time and battery stay current on the Lock Screen and Dynamic Island (Live Activity), even while you navigate in another app. It stops when you reach your destination or end the trip.
  • Drive log Auto-track (Pro, off by default): if you turn on Auto-track in Garage → Drives, JUICE uses significant location changes and visits, together with Motion & Fitness, to notice when a drive starts and ends, and uses GPS only during a drive. Drives are saved on your device.

You can change location access at any time in iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services → JUICE.

2. Data providers that receive locations or search areas

To answer a search (for example, "chargers along this route" or "weather at this stop"), JUICE sends the needed coordinates, route area or place name directly from your phone to the provider below. These requests do not include your name, email, or any JUICE identifier. Like any internet request, the provider can see your device's IP address. Each provider handles requests under its own privacy policy.

  • U.S. Department of Energy / National Renewable Energy Laboratory Alternative Fuels Station data (charger locations)
  • Open Charge Map (charger locations, ratings and check-ins)
  • OpenStreetMap / Overpass (chargers, amenities and roads)
  • Apple Maps / MapKit (maps, places, routes, map snapshots and Look Around images)
  • Open-Meteo (weather along your route)
  • Hong Kong government open data (chargers, roads and weather in Hong Kong)
  • Google Places (Pro only: live stall availability and places such as restaurants)
  • SerpApi (Pro only: hotel prices near a stop)
  • U.S. Energy Information Administration (gas price averages; JUICE only downloads public averages and sends no user data)

3. Motion & Fitness

JUICE uses motion activity (driving or not) only for Drive log Auto-track, to start and stop logging drives without draining your battery. It is processed on your device and not sent anywhere.

4. Tesla (optional, Pro)

  • You sign in on Tesla's own website in a secure Apple sign-in window. JUICE never sees your Tesla password.
  • To finish sign-in, Tesla returns a one-time code through a small callback page hosted on GitHub Pages, and that code is exchanged for access tokens through a small relay I run on Cloudflare Workers. The relay exists only to add JUICE's app secret so it is not stored inside the app. It forwards the request to Tesla and returns Tesla's answer to your phone. It does not store or log your code or tokens, and later token refreshes go straight from your phone to Tesla.
  • Your Tesla tokens are stored in the iOS Keychain on your device.
  • JUICE uses the Tesla Fleet API only while the app is open, to read your car's battery and charging state, location, odometer and navigation destination, and to send destinations to the car when you ask. If you turn on "Get ready for trips" (Pro), JUICE also sends the charge-limit and preconditioning commands you chose; these go through the same relay so they can be signed, your Tesla access token travels with that one request, and it is not stored or logged.
  • You can unlink your Tesla at any time in Garage. You can also revoke JUICE's access in your Tesla account.

5. Drive log (Pro)

Drives, routes, odometer readings, battery levels and tags are saved on your device. Exports (CSV or PDF) are created only when you choose to share them, and go wherever you send them. JUICE does not access your Photos library.

6. Apple Intelligence

"Ask JUICE" chat and drive-tag suggestions use Apple's on-device Foundation Models. JUICE does not send your questions or drive details to any server. Suggested tags are applied only after you approve them.

7. iCloud

If you use iCloud sync (Plus or Pro), your garage cars, learned car models, saved places, favorite chargers, saved trips and settings sync through your own iCloud account. Apple stores and handles this data under Apple's privacy policy; I cannot see it.

JUICE account: Sign in with Apple (app and website). If you choose "Sign in with Apple" in the app (Garage → Settings) or on the JUICE website (juiceev.app), your drives (with tags and notes), saved trips, tag rules, saved places, favorite chargers, drives saved to a calendar day, and your car names and plan are kept on JUICE's own server (Cloudflare, United States), so the app, your other devices and the website show the same things. They are linked to the private ID Apple gives JUICE for your Apple ID, plus your first name and email only if you choose to share them with Apple's sign-in. This data is used only to sync your account. It is never sold, never used for ads, and not shared with anyone except Cloudflare, which hosts it. It does not use your iCloud storage. Sign out at any time; your data stays on your devices. To delete the account and everything in it from the server, use Delete account in Garage → Settings, or email me. The website keeps its own copy in your browser until you clear it there.

Rating JUICE's lines (optional). If you turn on "Help write JUICE's lines" in the bot panel, your ratings, tags and any note you write for each test line are sent to JUICE's server with a random tester ID that is not linked to your account or name. They are used only to choose what the JUICE bot says.

Share ETA (optional). If you share your arrival time from a running trip, that trip's stop names, arrival times and arrival battery level are kept on JUICE's server behind the link you send (never your location) and are deleted within 48 hours.

Crash reports. If the app crashes, the report Apple's system makes on your device (where in the code it stopped, the app build, the system version and the device model; nothing about you or your trips) is sent to JUICE's server the next time you open the app, so I can fix it. Reports are deleted after 90 days.

8. Charger reviews

Reviews you write are saved on your device. The review form has a switch to also post the review to Open Charge Map. When that switch is on, your star rating, comment and the name you enter (or "JUICE driver" if you leave it blank) are posted publicly on Open Charge Map, where anyone can see them. Open Charge Map does not allow edits after posting. Turn the switch off to keep a review on your phone only.

9. Notifications and alarms

Notifications (for example, "charging done") and the optional leave-the-charger alarm are scheduled locally on your device. JUICE does not use a push server for them.

10. Purchases

  • JUICE Plus (one-time in-app purchase) and JUICE Pro (auto-renewing monthly subscription) are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. I never see your card details. Apple tells the app only which plan you have.
  • Sign up / Pay: this opens a charging network's own website (such as ChargePoint, EVgo, Blink or EV Connect) inside the app. Anything you enter there goes to that network under its own privacy policy, not to me. If you choose, JUICE can pre-fill car details saved on your device (such as VIN, license plate and color). JUICE never saves or fills in card numbers or passwords.

11. Children

JUICE is not directed to children under 13, and I do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

12. Keeping and deleting your data

  • Deleting JUICE from your iPhone removes the data saved on that device.
  • Unlink your Tesla in Garage to remove the Tesla tokens from your device.
  • iCloud copies can be removed in iPhone Settings → [your name] → iCloud.
  • If you use the JUICE account, "Delete my JUICE account" in the app (Garage → Settings) or on the website (Garage) removes its data from JUICE's server.
  • Reviews already posted to Open Charge Map are controlled by Open Charge Map.

13. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example, under the California Consumer Privacy Act or the EU/UK General Data Protection Regulation), you may have the right to know what personal information is processed about you, to access, correct or delete it, and to object to or limit its use. Unless you use the optional JUICE account, I do not hold your data on any server, so most of these rights are met by the controls on your own device. For any request or question, email hundavis10@gmail.com and I will respond within a reasonable time. You may also contact your local data protection authority.

14. Changes

If this policy changes, I will update this page and its effective date. Significant changes will also be noted in the app or its release notes.
